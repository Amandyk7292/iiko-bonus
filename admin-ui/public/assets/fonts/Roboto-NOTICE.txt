Bulka customer interface fonts

Roboto static subsets derived from Google Fonts:
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/roboto
License: Roboto-OFL.txt

The tenge currency glyph (U+20B8), absent from upstream Roboto, is sourced
from the bundled Montserrat fonts and scaled to Roboto's units per em.
License for that glyph: Montserrat-OFL.txt

Generated by scripts/prepare-roboto-fonts.py. Includes Latin and Cyrillic,
including Kazakh, punctuation and currency characters. No network font
service is required by the running application.
