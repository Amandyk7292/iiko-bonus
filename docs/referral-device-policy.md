# Referral eligibility

Invitations and rewards have no daily, monthly or per-code quantity ceilings. Reward amounts and minimum first purchase remain configurable. Zero-valued legacy settings represent no ceiling.

An installation is assigned to the first customer recorded for it. Additional accounts can sign in and buy, but neither invitee nor inviter rewards can be paid to them on that installation. Both beneficiaries must have device evidence. Manual approval cannot bypass the device check. Missing evidence holds payment until the customer opens the referral page. Refunds still reverse both rewards through existing bonus debt accounting. Device claims survive account deletion and refunds; existing paid rewards are not retrospectively revoked.

Security limitation: current evidence is a client-supplied random installation ID stored in preferences, not a hardware identity or device attestation. Reinstallation, cleared app/browser storage or a modified client can change it. This provides account-switch protection on a recorded installation, not an absolute one-physical-phone guarantee. Native stable identifiers and platform attestation require a separate mobile release and platform integration.
