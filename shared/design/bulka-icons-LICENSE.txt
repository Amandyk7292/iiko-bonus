Canonical geometry: shared/design/bulka-icons.json
Generator: scripts/generate-bulka-ui-icons.py
Dependencies: Python 3.10+, scripts/bulka-icon-generator-requirements.txt

Generate:
  python scripts/generate-bulka-ui-icons.py

Verify the committed assets reproduce exactly:
  python scripts/generate-bulka-ui-icons.py --check

Optional visual review sheet:
  python scripts/generate-bulka-ui-icons.py --contact-sheet outputs/icons.svg

Flutter declares the app fonts with compatibility families MaterialIcons and
packages/cupertino_icons/CupertinoIcons. Their artwork remains Bulka's own.
Material aliases and Cupertino aliases are separate codepoint namespaces.

The generator is offline and deterministic. It preserves the 24-unit drawing
grid, rounds stroke ends and joins, converts strokes to font outlines, and
keeps the checked/unchecked, filled/outline, and enabled/off meanings distinct.
